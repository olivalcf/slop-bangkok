## Design Context

### Users
People exploring a playable 3D Bangkok in a browser. Walk, drive, cycle, fly, and jump between landmarks.

### Brand Personality
Playful, exploratory, cinematic. A Bangkok remix of mrdoob's Slop New York.

### Aesthetic Direction
The user chose to stay close to the original playable city. Preserve the restrained corner HUD and full-screen city. Use warm gold accents, Bangkok street names, metric telemetry, and bilingual place labels.

### Design Principles
- The city occupies the screen; controls stay at its edges.
- Keep the original movement and camera interactions.
- Use Bangkok geography and distinctive architecture, not relabeled Manhattan geometry.
- Label procedural detail and geographic coverage honestly.
- Keep touch controls and keyboard navigation usable.
